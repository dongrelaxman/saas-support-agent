# Mobile Apps

TaskPilot has mobile apps for iOS and Android.

## Requirements
iOS 15 or later. Android 9 or later.

## Offline mode
The mobile apps cache your recent projects so you can view and edit tasks without a connection. Offline changes sync automatically when you reconnect. Cached data is kept for up to 7 days offline.

## Push notifications
Enable notifications in Settings > Notifications. You can mute individual projects. If you do not receive notifications, check that background refresh is enabled in your phone settings.
