# Billing and Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, and PayPal. Business and Enterprise customers can also pay by invoice with net-30 terms.

## Changing your payment method
Go to Settings > Billing > Payment method and choose Update. Changes apply to the next charge.

## Failed payments
If a payment fails, we retry 3 times over 7 days and email the workspace Owner each time.
If all retries fail, the workspace is downgraded to the Free plan. Data over the Free plan limits becomes read-only but is not deleted for 30 days.

## Invoices
Invoices are available under Settings > Billing > Invoices as PDF. You can add a VAT or tax ID in Settings > Billing > Company details. Invoices cannot be edited after they are issued.
