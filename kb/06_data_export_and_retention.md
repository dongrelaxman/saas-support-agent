# Data Export and Retention

## Exporting your data
Go to Settings > Data > Export and select CSV or JSON. Large workspaces can take up to 24 hours to process. We email a download link that stays valid for 7 days.
Only Owners and Admins can export workspace data.

## Trash and deleted items
Deleted tasks and projects stay in the Trash for 30 days and can be restored by Admins. After 30 days they are permanently deleted.

## Data hosting
Data is hosted on AWS. Workspaces are created in us-east-1 by default. Business and Enterprise workspaces can choose the EU region (eu-west-1) at creation time. The region cannot be changed afterwards.

## Compliance
TaskPilot is SOC 2 Type II certified. Our latest report is available to Business and Enterprise customers under NDA. We are GDPR compliant and offer a Data Processing Agreement on request.
