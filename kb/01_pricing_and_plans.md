# TaskPilot Pricing and Plans

TaskPilot offers four plans. Prices are per user per month in USD.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Projects | Users | Storage |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | $0 | 3 | up to 5 | 2 GB |
| Pro | $12 | $10 | Unlimited | Unlimited | 100 GB |
| Business | $24 | $20 | Unlimited | Unlimited | 1 TB |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Custom |

Annual billing is charged once per year and saves roughly 17 percent compared with monthly billing.
Enterprise pricing is quoted by our sales team. Contact sales@taskpilot.example.

## Free trial
Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of the Pro plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.
When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless a paid plan is selected.

## Upload limits
Maximum single file upload size is 250 MB on Free and 2 GB on Pro and Business.
