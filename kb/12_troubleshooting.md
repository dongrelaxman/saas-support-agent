# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Tasks not loading | Browser cache or extension conflict | Hard refresh (Ctrl+Shift+R) or try a private window |
| Cannot upload file | File exceeds plan limit | Check the upload limit for your plan (250 MB Free, 2 GB Pro and Business) |
| Slack notifications missing | Integration disconnected | Reconnect under Settings > Integrations > Slack |
| Invite email not received | Spam filter or typo | Resend from Members > Invite; invitations expire after 7 days |
| "Workspace is read-only" banner | Payment failed or plan limit exceeded | Update payment method in Settings > Billing |
| Calendar view empty | Tasks have no due dates | Add due dates to tasks |
