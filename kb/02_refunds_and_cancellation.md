# Refunds and Cancellation

## Refund policy
Annual plans can be refunded within 14 days of the first purchase. Refunds are issued to the original payment method and take 5 to 10 business days to appear.
Monthly plans are not refundable, but you can cancel at any time and you will not be charged again.
Renewals of annual plans are not eligible for refunds. We send a reminder email 7 days before renewal.

## How to cancel
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel plan. Your workspace stays on the paid plan until the end of the current billing period, then moves to the Free plan.
Only workspace Owners and Billing Admins can cancel a subscription.

## Deleting your account
Account deletion is available under Settings > Account > Delete account. Deleted accounts have a 30-day grace period during which you can sign in and restore the account.
After 30 days all data is permanently removed and cannot be recovered.
