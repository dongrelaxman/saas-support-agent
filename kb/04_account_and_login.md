# Account and Login Help

## Resetting your password
Select "Forgot password" on the sign-in page and enter your email. The reset link is valid for 30 minutes. If it expires, request a new one.
Check your spam folder if the email does not arrive within 5 minutes.

## Account lockout
After 5 failed sign-in attempts your account is locked for 15 minutes. Resetting your password does not remove the lockout early.

## Changing your email
Go to Settings > Profile > Email. We send a confirmation link to the new address. The change is applied once you click it.

## Inviting team members
Open Members > Invite and enter email addresses. Roles are Admin, Member, and Guest. Guests can only see projects they are added to, and up to 10 guests are free on every plan.
Invitations expire after 7 days.
